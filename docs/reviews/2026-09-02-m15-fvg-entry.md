## Implemented rule

`apex_ai/scalper/m15_fvg_entry.py` supplies one shared M15 entry evaluator to the live scalper and backtest. It replaces the legacy M5 FVG detector while `--m15-fvg-entry` is enabled; a rejected M15 plan cannot fall back to the old FVG detector. Within the FVG family, a valid M15 zone entry takes priority over a local sweep. Other enabled trigger families retain their existing checks.

1. Form a three-candle **M15** FVG using completed, consecutive candles and a directional displacement body. Reuse L-017's declared thresholds: preceding ATR(14), body >=0.8 ATR and >=60% of range, close in the outer 25%, gap >=0.05 ATR, lifetime 24 M15 bars.
2. Enter only while the actual executable quote is inside that gap. The completed M15 formation establishes the setup; the live quote supplies the touch entry. Do not substitute the latest candle's historical low or an earlier signal close as the fill. This uses the current scan loop; it does not place resting limit orders and can miss brief intrabar touches.
3. Place the stop beyond the distal gap boundary by **0.30 × formation ATR**, retaining the existing FVG stop buffer. Do not tighten it to make the proposed target pass.
4. Place TP **before the nearest already-broken M15 swing level in the profit direction**, using L-017's same confirmed-pivot/break definitions and frozen zone buffer. No structural target means no trade. A nearer obstacle is not skipped to obtain a better R multiple.
5. Require **gross reward/risk >=2** plus the existing spread, minimum-stop and net-cost gates. The final quote must still be inside the gap, offer at least 2R, and be no worse than the quote used for sizing; costs are checked again immediately before submission.
6. A full distal-boundary mitigation consumes the gap. Partial visits do not, but reusing it after a position closes requires a completed departure from the zone after that close before another touch. Close history restores across restarts. Missing candle history fails closed.
7. Apply the symmetric short rule. Existing session, news, bias, regime, risk, cooldown and position limits still apply. L-017 still blocks a trade whose proposed path goes through unreclaimed broken structure. A trade that exits before that structure is a different path and needs no breakout through its target.

Orders carry `SA_FVG_M15`. **TGA keeps their structural TP fixed**, including after a restart, instead of extending it through resistance. Stop trailing and protective early exits remain active. The backtest Guardian receives the same extension restriction. The setup uses one structural target, so TP2 equals TP1 and the macro TP2 override is suppressed for this model.

Default enabled; configuration era **`2026-09-02-m15-fvg-target`**. Disable only this model with `--no-m15-fvg-entry`. To reproduce the entry contract before both September 2 changes, also disable L-017 with `--no-reclaim-fvg`. Neither historical chart price is hardcoded in production.
